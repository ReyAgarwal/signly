Learn to fingerspell in ASL

Hold your hand up to the webcam. The coach recognizes the letter you're signing, tells you which finger to fix, and tracks your progress. Everything runs on this laptop: no internet needed, and no video ever leaves the computer.

✓

The video is mirrored like a selfie. Nothing is recorded or uploaded; only 21 hand points are saved when you record samples.

Record Samples

Pick a letter, make the sign, then press Record. The coach saves one snapshot of your hand every tenth of a second for 3 seconds.

A

Tips for good samples
  • Record about 30-60 samples per letter. More is better.
  • Move your hand a little while recording: slightly closer, farther, and tilted.
  • Have two or more teammates record, and try both hands.
  • Use good lighting and keep your whole hand inside the video.

Accuracy test

The test hides the newest 20% of each letter's samples, then asks the k-NN classifier to recognize them using only the other 80%. It shows the overall accuracy and which letters get mixed up.
